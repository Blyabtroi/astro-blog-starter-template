Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

BeatBob is a music sidekick: your character nods to nearby music heard through the mic. Here's the short version before the details.

The short version

  • Mic is on only while Listen is active. We don't save recordings.
  • Beat detection runs on your iPhone.
  • Shazam (optional) may send audio signatures to Apple to ID a track.
  • The app is free and shows Yandex ads. Ad partners may collect device/ad data.
  • We don't do cross-app tracking.

Microphone

When you tap Listen, BeatBob uses the mic to hear music in the room and drive the character. Processing is live on-device. Raw audio isn't stored or uploaded by BeatBob.

ShazamKit

On a real iPhone with network, the app may use Apple ShazamKit to recognize a song and hint a genre. See Apple's privacy policy.

Custom skins

Photos or PNGs you import for skins are processed on-device and stored locally in the app's sandbox.

Ads & analytics

Yandex Mobile Ads may collect device identifiers and advertising data for ad delivery and SDK analytics. Not linked to your identity in BeatBob, not used for tracking by us. Yandex privacy

What we don't collect

  • Accounts or logins
  • Location, contacts, health data
  • Cross-app tracking on our side

Kids

Not aimed at children under 13.

Your controls

  • Mic: Settings → BeatBob → Microphone
  • Ads tracking: iOS Privacy & Security → Tracking
