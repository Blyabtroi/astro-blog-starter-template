Privacy Policy

Mobile Application «Kino Naoborot»

1. Introduction

Welcome to the «Kino Naoborot» application (hereinafter — the Application). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

The Application is developed by Vasiliy Kozlov, a self-employed individual (self‑employed status in the Russian Federation). By using the Application, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with the terms, please do not use the Application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: When you first launch the Application, we generate a unique user ID and a nickname. No email or password is required.
  • User Content: Your guesses (movies you guess), game results, and any other content you create while playing.
  • Support Communications: If you contact us by email, we may keep your messages and contact details to assist you.

2.2. Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifier (device ID), language settings, screen resolution.
  • Usage Data: Game progress, scores, streaks, number of games played, features used.
  • Push Notification Tokens: If you allow push notifications, we receive a device token to send you information about new games and challenges.

2.3. Advertising Data (Yandex Ads)

  • The Application uses Yandex Advertising services to display ads. Yandex may collect data in accordance with its own Privacy Policy.
  • We do not collect IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers) and do not share additional information with ad networks beyond what is necessary to serve advertisements.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Operate and improve the Application, track game progress and maintain leaderboards;
  • Personalize game content and ensure fair play;
  • Send push notifications about new games and events (you can disable them in device settings);
  • Respond to your questions and support requests;
  • Prevent technical issues, fraud, and violations of our rules;
  • Display advertisements through the Yandex Advertising network;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. In limited circumstances, data may be shared with:

  • Advertising Partners: Yandex Ads receives technical data necessary to serve advertisements, as described in their privacy policy.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, court order, or lawful request from public authorities.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, we will notify you through the Application or other means.

5. Data Storage and Security

  • Location: Your information is stored on servers located in Moscow, Russian Federation.
  • Security Measures: We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.
  • Retention Period: We retain your data as long as you use the Application. After you delete your account, your information is anonymized or deleted within 30 days, unless retention is required by law or to prevent fraud.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and Correction: You may request access to or correction of your personal data by contacting us at blyabtroi@gmail.com.
  • Account Deletion: You can delete your account through the Application settings (Profile → Log Out / Delete Account) or by contacting our support.
  • Push Notifications: You can disable push notifications in your device settings (Settings → Notifications → Kino Naoborot).

7. Children

The Application is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required by your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us, and we will delete it.

8. International Data Transfers

If you are located outside the Russian Federation, please note that your information may be transferred to and processed in Russia, where our servers are located. By using the Application, you consent to such transfer. We take steps to ensure an adequate level of protection for your data in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

9. Links to Third‑Party Services

The Application may contain links to third‑party websites or services (e.g., Yandex Ads, our support email). This Privacy Policy does not apply to those services. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the Application or other means. Your continued use of the Application after the changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
